Welcome to a minimalist news aggregator built for signal over noise. By stripping away engagement bait, comment sections and visual clutter, {{ brand }} lets you focus on what actually matters: the stories shaping the day.

{{ brand }} aggregates articles from a curated list of RSS feeds, scores them based on title and description similarity across publications, and surfaces the top story from each domain in a calm, distraction-free interface.

Lucian Marin created {{ brand }} for busy people who don't want to spend more than a few minutes getting the most important news of the day without algorithmic manipulation or endless doomscrolling.

Install as a web app

  • Install on Android by adding it to your home screen from Chrome, or use the native Android client.
  • Install on iPhone or iPad by adding it to your home screen from Safari.
  • Also available on Windows, Linux and natively on macOS.

Tech stack and support

  1. Hosted on a high-frequency Vultr VPS running Ubuntu 24.04 LTS.
  2. Built with Python 3.12, FastAPI, Jinja2 and Tortoise ORM.
  3. Powered by Uvicorn behind Nginx and backed by SQLite with aiosqlite.
  4. RSS aggregation with aiohttp, feedparser and BeautifulSoup; similarity scoring with scikit-learn and SciPy.
  5. Icons by Heroicons and Sidekickicons, Route 159 typeface by Sora Sagano.
  6. Donate via PayPal to keep {{ brand }} ad-free and running indefinitely.

Features and design constraints

  1. Curated RSS aggregation across {{ sites | length }} tech, science, culture and news publications.
  2. TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity scoring across article titles and descriptions.
  3. Hottest view ranks stories covered across multiple publications by highest similarity score.
  4. Coldest view highlights unique stories with the lowest similarity score across publications.
  5. Newest view presents the latest stories in reverse-chronological order.
  6. One top story per domain in main feeds so no single publication dominates the page.
  7. Dedicated per-publication feeds showing all recent stories from a specific source.
  8. Automatic cleanup of articles older than 48 hours to keep every feed fresh.
  9. Concise summaries truncated to 480 characters with temperature scores in degrees.
  10. Automatic light and dark themes following your OS preference.
